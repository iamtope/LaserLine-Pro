# Privacy Policy for Laserline Pro

**Last updated: $(date +"%B %d, %Y")**

## Camera Access
- We use camera access solely for laser leveling functionality
- Camera data is processed locally on your device
- No camera data is stored, transmitted, or shared
- Camera access is optional - app works without it

## Data Collection
- We do not collect personal information
- Sensor data (gyroscope, accelerometer) is processed locally
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- All measurements and calculations happen on your device

## Third-Party Services
- Google AdMob: Displays advertisements (may collect anonymous usage data)
- No other third-party data collection

## Contact
For questions about this privacy policy, contact: support@laserlinepro.com

## Changes
We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
